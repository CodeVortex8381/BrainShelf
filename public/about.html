WHO WE ARE

More than just a notes platform.

BrainShelf is designed to bring students and educational resources together in one place. Whether you are preparing for examinations, learning a new programming language or revising important concepts, BrainShelf helps you discover useful study materials shared by the student community. Our goal is to create a simple and convenient learning environment where students can learn from one another.

OUR MISSION

Making learning resources accessible.

Our mission is to make sharing and discovering educational resources simple, convenient and accessible for students. We aim to encourage students to share their knowledge and help one another succeed.

OUR VISION

A community where knowledge grows.

We envision a learning community where students help one another by sharing knowledge, ideas and useful educational resources. We want to make collaborative learning a natural part of student life.
